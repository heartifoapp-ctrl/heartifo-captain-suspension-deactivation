Captain Suspension & Deactivation Policy

Account review, suspension and deactivation guidelines

1. Purpose

This policy explains the circumstances in which Heartifo may temporarily restrict, suspend, deactivate or terminate a Captain account.

These measures help protect customers, Captains, the platform and the integrity of Heartifo services.

2. Temporary Account Restriction

Heartifo may temporarily restrict access to certain Captain features while an account, ride, document, payment activity or reported incident is being reviewed.

A restriction may remain in place until the relevant review has been completed or required information has been provided.

3. Reasons for Suspension

A Captain account may be suspended or restricted in situations including:

  • Safety-related concerns
  • Suspected fraudulent activity
  • False or misleading account information
  • Invalid, expired or rejected documents
  • Serious or repeated customer complaints
  • Repeated cancellation or no-show activity
  • Payment or earnings-related abuse
  • Violation of Heartifo policies
  • Use of another person's account
  • Other activity requiring platform review

4. Immediate Safety Action

Where Heartifo receives information indicating a potential immediate safety risk, access to ride-related features may be restricted while the matter is reviewed.

Serious incidents may require additional information or verification before access is restored.

5. Document-Related Restrictions

Captain services may be restricted when required documents are missing, expired, rejected, unverifiable or otherwise do not meet applicable requirements.

Captains may be required to submit updated documents before access to certain services is restored.

6. Fraud and Platform Abuse

Heartifo may suspend or deactivate accounts involved in suspected fraud, manipulation or misuse of the platform.

  • Fake rides or ride activity
  • False GPS or location information
  • Fake or fraudulent documents
  • Account sharing or impersonation
  • Manipulation of promotions or incentives
  • Unauthorized attempts to obtain earnings
  • Other attempts to manipulate Heartifo systems

7. Customer Complaints

Heartifo may review customer complaints relating to Captain conduct, safety, service quality, communication or other platform-related concerns.

A complaint does not automatically establish that a violation occurred. Heartifo may review available information before taking account action.

8. Investigation and Verification

During a review, Heartifo may request information or documents from the Captain that are reasonably necessary to understand the matter.

Captains are expected to cooperate honestly with reasonable verification and support requests.

9. Account Deactivation

A Captain account may be deactivated when Heartifo determines that continued access is not appropriate under applicable platform rules or requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, deactivation may be temporary or permanent.

10. Serious Violations

Serious violations may result in stronger account action, including immediate suspension or permanent deactivation, where appropriate.

This may include serious safety violations, fraudulent documents, impersonation, violence, harassment or other prohibited conduct.

11. Captain Account Access

A Captain must not create or use another account to bypass a suspension, restriction or deactivation.

Attempts to bypass account restrictions may result in additional account action.

12. Review or Appeal

If a Captain believes that an account restriction or deactivation was made incorrectly, the Captain may contact Heartifo Support and provide relevant information for review.

Heartifo may request additional information before completing the review.

13. Earnings and Outstanding Amounts

Suspension or deactivation does not automatically cancel legitimate amounts that are otherwise payable or outstanding.

Any payment, adjustment, dispute or recovery will be handled according to the applicable Heartifo policies and the circumstances of the account.

14. Account Deletion by Captain

Captains may request deletion of their account through the available Heartifo support process.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legitimate purposes.

15. Policy Changes

Heartifo may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, applicable requirements or operational practices.

16. Contact Heartifo

For questions, account reviews, suspension concerns or deactivation-related support, please contact Heartifo.
